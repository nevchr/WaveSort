# Brag Plan: WaveSort

## Inspection rubric

1. WaveSort is a local-first Windows tool that scans sample folders, suggests explainable sound categories, and copies or moves a reviewed selection into organized folders.
2. Strongest honest claim: “Copy leaves originals in place.” This makes the sort approachable without overstating the filename/folder-cue classifier.
3. Visual hook: a messy list of sample filenames resolving into the near-black WaveSort review table, with warm gold category labels.
4. Show the real UI structure and wording: “Sort your sounds.”, “Your sounds”, review rows and detail pane, copy-plan preview, operation progress.
5. About 21 seconds is enough for input → review → approved copy → result.
6. Preset: polished. Direction: quiet, precise tool film; substantial motion only where it clarifies the workflow.
7. Audio: restrained rhythmic bed, a few quiet click/reveal accents, no voice.
8. Caption: “Made WaveSort to turn a messy sample folder into reviewable Kicks, Snares, Hats, Vox, and more. Copy first; originals stay put.”
9. User flow: choose a source folder → review and correct the suggested categories → inspect a copy plan and copy approved files into category folders.

## What is this app?

A local-first sample organizer that makes a messy audio folder reviewable and sorts approved files into category folders.

## The angle

The satisfaction of going from “where is that snare?” to a clean library, without surrendering control of the files. The film shows a real WaveSort-shaped tool, not abstract sound waves or unsupported audio-analysis claims. Demo filenames and counts are illustrative, not taken from a user's private library.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

“Your samples. Everywhere.” Three plausible filenames drift into a loose stack; a crisp gold rule resolves them into the WaveSort interface.

## Key moments

- “Choose a folder” initiates a scan; a few filenames populate the actual-style review table.
- Category rows resolve as Kicks, Snares, Hats, Vox; one row is visibly changed by hand, showing that guesses are reviewable.
- The “Copy plan” preview shows nested destinations and an explicit “Copy verified plan” action; progress reaches completion.

## Outro / punchline

“Sorted. Originals untouched.” WaveSort wordmark and the small “Local only” cue hold cleanly.

## User flow worth showing

Choose folder → review categories → approve copy plan → organized output.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Unsorted — 2.5s

Headline “Your samples. Everywhere.” over a restrained stack of illustrative audio filenames. Gold line establishes WaveSort identity. Hold headline fully visible at least 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: three filenames appear quickly, then hold together.
Audio intent: warm, slight tension.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft reveal, not a sound per filename.
Music: subdued rhythmic bed.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Choose and scan — 4.5s

Recreate WaveSort empty state with verbatim “Sort your sounds.” and “Choose a folder”; visible cursor selects the button. The view becomes the real-style “Your sounds” table with plausible demo filenames. Keep enough time to read the button and title.
Sequential/interaction: click, then four file rows appear in order.
Audio intent: the tool feels responsive, not magical.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet click, soft row arrivals at musically convenient intervals.
Music: steady low bed.
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Review — 7s

Show actual-style review table and detail pane. Readable labels Kicks, Snares, Hats, Vox. One uncertain row is selected and corrected via Category control. Copy conveys that classification is a suggestion based on cues, not waveform recognition.
Sequential/interaction: categories settle one by one; cursor changes one category and holds the corrected state.
Audio intent: calm control.
Audio-coupled idea: selection click and one restrained confirmation.
Music: steady low bed.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Review, then copy — 4.5s

Show WaveSort-style “Copy plan” with destination preview, then “Copy verified plan”. Progress fills to 100%. Do not suggest copying happens without explicit approval.
Sequential/interaction: button click, progress, completion.
Audio intent: reassuring finish.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet click and soft success cue near 15.82s if timing permits.
Music: slight lift, then recede.
Transition mood: soft fade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — WaveSort — 2.5s

“Sorted. Originals untouched.” with WaveSort wordmark and “Local only”. Full final line readable for at least 1.5s.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: resolved, unobtrusive.
Audio-coupled idea: single subtle logo accent near 18.55s if timing permits.
Music: fade to silence.
Transition mood: end.
